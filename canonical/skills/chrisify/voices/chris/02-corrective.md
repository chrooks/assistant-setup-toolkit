# Chris — Corrective Scan Checklist

Run this scan over every draft before delivering. Fix violations in place.
The anti-AI baseline is NOT here — run the installed `stop-slop` and `humanizer`
skills for that; this file holds only Chris-specific caps.

## Hard caps (per ~700 words)

- [ ] Em-dashes: ≤1 occurrence (paired counts as one); never as a clause splice
- [ ] Semicolons: 0 outside serial lists
- [ ] Boosters and absolutes (clearly, certainly, very, always, never, must): ~0 — Chris qualifies instead of absolutizing
- [ ] No staccato runs — never two consecutive sentences under 8 words
- [ ] Exclamation points: 0 in essay/journal registers

## Shape checks

- [ ] Mean sentence length in range for the register (essay 24–31, speech 20–27, journal 21–27)
- [ ] At least one concession construction per ~7 sentences (but/however/though/that isn't to say)
- [ ] Parenthetical asides present (~1 per 140 words in essay/journal)
- [ ] First person appears where the register allows it — never scrubbed to passive academic voice
- [ ] No announcement colons ("The result: X")
- [ ] Contractions preserved — Chris writes "it'd", "doesn't", "who'd"
- [ ] No clause-stacked welds: any sentence carrying 3+ independent claims joined
      by and/because gets split — a long sentence must climb to a payoff, not circle
      _(2026-08-28)_
- [ ] Say-it-once: no claim restated in slightly different words after a citation
      or across adjacent paragraphs — the quote-sandwich circling of the 2021
      corpus is disowned _(2026-08-28)_
- [ ] The short hammer: a load-bearing point may land as a single ≤8-word
      sentence; the no-staccato-runs cap above still stands

## Voice checks

- [ ] At least one colloquial connector or aside where length permits
  (Needless to say / look no further than / the list goes on / a parenthetical joke)
- [ ] Imagery is self-coined and concrete, not stock metaphor
- [ ] Humor undercuts formality somewhere in longer pieces — never forced into short ones
