# Apply to a Job: setup

**What it is:** Instructions that turn ChatGPT into your job-application helper. You paste a job link. You get back a resume and a cover letter made for that job.

**Why you care:** Every application gets a resume and letter that match the posting, in a few minutes, without starting from scratch.

## Do this once

1. Double-click the zip file. You get a folder with `SKILL.md`, `render.py`, and this file.
2. Open ChatGPT. In the left sidebar, click **New project**. Name it **Job Applications**.
3. In the project, add three files: `SKILL.md`, `render.py`, and your current resume (the PDF).
4. Open the project's instructions and paste this line:

   > When I share a job posting or ask for help applying to a job, follow SKILL.md exactly.

## Every time you apply

1. Open the **Job Applications** project and start a new chat.
2. Paste the job link and type: **help me apply to this**
3. Download the two PDFs it gives you.
4. Answer the **Check before you send** questions before you submit.

## If something goes wrong

- **It says it can't open the page:** copy the whole job description from the site and paste it into the chat.
- **It says it can't find render.py:** drag `render.py` into the chat.
- **It says it can't make PDFs:** it gives you the text instead. Paste it into a Google Doc or Word file.
- **New resume:** delete the old one from the project's files and add the new one.

## Good to know

- It only uses what's on your resume. It won't make anything up.
- If it calls a job a long shot, that's an honest read. Applying is still your call.

## Using Claude instead

In Claude, go to **Customize**, then **Skills**. Click **+**, choose **Upload a skill**, and pick the zip file. Then paste a job link in any chat.
